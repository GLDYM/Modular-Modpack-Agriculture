## Introduction

Some mods are only served as a necessary component in the modpacks that focus on Tech or Adventure, or just distributing friends, or used for a public server. The modpack creator cannot put too much effort into the mods that are not important or unfamiliar with. This makes them not perform so well in the modpack.

Modular Modpack focuses on a specific theme, solves the compatibility issues, and unifies duplicate content between mods that have similar content. It provides a portable solution to reduce the pressure on the modpack creators. 

Modular Modpack is not meant to be used directly, but acts as a part of other modpacks that are distributed to friends, published publicly, or used for public servers.

## How to use?

Just fork this project, or install this project, and then move the necessary files into your modpack. Notice that this modpack needs some Lib mods to build, so you need to remove duplicate mods.

If you just want to play these mods, you need a basic modpack like Ultramodern Optimization or Asai Integrated Modpack, and copy all files into this basic modpack.

## Mod Standard

### General Standard

- Published on Curseforge or Modrinth.
- Not created by MCreator.
- Open Source, ARR mod should validate the stability.
- Active Maintenance is better.

### Modular Standard

- KubeJS & Rhino.
- Have agriculture content or necessary Lib mod.
- Mods dependent on non-agriculture or Lib mod will not be acceptable, such as Twilight's Flavors & Delight.
